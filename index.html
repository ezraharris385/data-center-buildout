Data Center Buildout

An interactive 3D teaching studio for how data centers actually work — built to exact catalog dimensions (millimeter-accurate, from real manufacturer datasheets).

The four archetypes

Hyperscale — single-tenant AI factory. Liquid-cooled NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 racks at ~120 kW each, row CDUs, massive gray-space UPS lineups, and a yard full of generators, chillers and thermal storage.

Cloud — a cloud availability-zone hall. Mixed OCP ORv3 and EIA racks, air + liquid cooling, modular UPS strings that scale with demand.

Colocation — multi-tenant retail colo. Caged customer zones, mixed rack sizes, raised floor, perimeter CRAH cooling, redundant utility feeds.

Enterprise — the on-prem server room. A handful of racks, one UPS, one generator: where most IT actually started.

What to try

• Kill the utility feed and watch the ATS transfer to generators.
• Push IT load to 100% and watch the heat plumes and PUE respond.
• Turn off the roof and read the airflow: cold aisle → servers → hot aisle → return.
• Click a GB200 rack, a chiller, a UPS — every object carries its real spec sheet.
